# Decision ticket

Used in step 1, and for any big choice later (a data source, a platform, a core rule). It holds the decider's OK.

```markdown
Title: Decide: <the question, in one line>

Question
<What we are deciding and why now.>

Options
1. <Option> — cost, risks, licence or terms
2. <Option> — cost, risks, licence or terms

AI analysis
<Summary of the practical analysis.>

Persona views
- Brand: <view>
- UX: <view>

Decision
<Go / Park / Drop>, decided by <name> on <date>.

If this is an MVP shortcut
Temporary. Replace when: <condition>.

Why
<The reason, in two or three sentences. This is what future readers search for.>
```
