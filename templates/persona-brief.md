# Persona brief

Used in step 1. One brief per persona. It says what the persona judges, not how to prompt it.

```markdown
Persona: <role, e.g. Brand expert / UX designer>

Judges
<What this persona looks at: tone and identity / ease of use and flow / ...>

Asks
- <A question it always asks, e.g. "Who is this for, and what do they feel?">
- <Another>

Never decides
The persona advises. The decider decides.
```
